The Battle for Wesnoth 1.20
Azure Expanses Update

November 2026. The development team is proud to present version 1.20 of The Battle for Wesnoth!

This release has a variety of major and minor changes to all parts of the game from multiple new and revised campaigns, an overhauled UI, a variety of new multiplayer features, improved graphical performance, new functionality for content creators, a new Android port, and even more! Try out the new features for yourself, or read on for more details...

What’s New in This Update

For Players

Wesnoth once again has an Android port, currently available on the F-Droid store, representing both a lot of work and the first time in many years that a modern version of Wesnoth is available outside of the usual Windows, macOS, and Linux systems. It is, however, still very much a work in progress and due to UI constraints will work better on tablets than phones.

Performance has been further improved, with graphical lag and stuttering significantly reduced especially on lower end systems.

Additionally, with this release the new Celes UI theme has been added containing a major overhaul of the main menu to give Wesnoth a more interesting and modern look, the in-game help browser now also has a search and collapsible side-bar to help make it easier to find the information you're looking for, and the reach-map showing how far units can move and who they can attack has been redone to provide clearer information while also being highly configurable. The previous theme, dubbed ‘Parchment’ still exists and both can be switched via display preferences.

Campaigns

Heir to the Throne now features an open world with 33 branching scenarios, each awarding Konrad unique recruits, companions, or items. The original campaign is still available as Heir to the Throne, Classic.

The Deceiver’s Gambit replaces Delfador’s Memoirs. Follow a young mage from the shores of Alduin to the halls of the King as they war against the fierce orcs of the north. Choose between 23 magic spells as you bear witness to the rise of a tyrant.

Dusk of Dawn is our first troll campaign. Lead the Golden Flame Tribe out from the bowels of Irdya and up to the surface world — but beware what awaits you there.

Of Pearls and Pirates is a new beginner-oriented campaign set in a sleepy fishing town on the Bay of Pearls. When corsairs strike, the villagers must join forces with the local merfolk and take the fight to the pirates.

The South Guard has been significantly revised, and now replaces the classic Battle Training tutorial. For players nostalgic for Delfador's tutelage, the original tutorial is still available as part of Heir to the Throne, Classic.

The Hammer of Thursagan has been enhanced with story adjustments, improved enemy AI, a new Thunderer advancement, a redesign of High Pass, and the re-addition of the formerly removed Mages and Drakes.

Winds of Fate has been re-balanced with much lower recall costs, redesigned wildlife spawns and AI, and a much larger mid-campaign battle. Several characters and dialogue sequences were changed for better continuity with other campaigns.

Multiplayer

Online multiplayer also had just as many changes with the addition of an official tournaments and ranked mode web site where players can compete in individual tournaments, for Elo in the ranking system, send challenges to individual players, and much more. In support of this there are now queues in the online lobby that players can join that will automatically start a game once there are enough players as well as a planned Ranked Era official add-on that will be updated more frequently with unit balance changes between 1.20 and 1.22.

Speaking of, another round of balancing changes have been implemented for the Default era factions, which now includes the Dunefolk. Along with this, several scenarios have been updated to better account for the Dunefolk as well as address some long standing issues in certain faction match-ups. A new scenario, Double Siege, has also been added.

All of this will allow us to have tools to easily host various events and tournaments through the span of this version and beyond. This will hopefully let us bring more engagement and players and eras to the scene, recovering a sliver of well deserved glory this game should have!

For Content Creators

And last but not least, there have been a variety of improvements for content creators as well with the most obvious being the new unit type editor that's been added to the scenario editor. With this it's possible to create entire new units from within the editor itself.

For more advanced uses, it's now also possible for add-on authors to define their own GUI2 themes and use them within their own content via the lua function gui.switch_theme() or publish them as standalone Wesnoth themes.

Many other APIs have been implemented and improved as well including an alignment property for individual weapons, many new features for abilities and weapon specials including a registry of both to make using them much easier, new functionality added to lua widgets to allow for more complex dialogues, the ability to define custom recruit and recall dialogues, and at long last simultaneous movement of units with [move_units_fake].

The wmllint tool has been updated to address some long standing issues and is also now regularly run against the base game which will help to keep it in a maintained and working state for add-on authors.

Contribute

The Battle for Wesnoth is constantly being improved and expanded upon by a multitude of international volunteers from diverse backgrounds. Since its inception in July 2003, hundreds of people have contributed to all areas of the game, from mainline content shipped as part of the official releases to hundreds of player-made projects available to download from our add-ons server. Whether you need help beating a particular scenario or adding a feature to your custom maps, Wesnoth boasts a massive community of players and creators always willing to help.

Besides creating your own add-on content, there are many other ways you can help Wesnoth:

  • Contribute code
  • Create art for the main game or for user-made add-ons
  • Translate the game into your language
  • Produce new music tracks and sound effects
  • Spread the word about Wesnoth on social media!
